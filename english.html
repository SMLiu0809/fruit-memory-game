👨‍🏫 About the Designer
Developed by Prof. Shih-Min Liu (NKNU). This game perfectly integrates the rigorous spirit of qualitative research, educational psychology, and AI generative technology. Dedicated to creating high-quality digital educational tools that are "enjoyable for all ages and eye-friendly."
🎯 Mission:
1. Memorize the positions of the fruits before they flip.
2. Once the cards are faced down, flip them open two at a time.
3. Match identical fruits to score! Clear levels to earn Health Points and upgrade your rank!
